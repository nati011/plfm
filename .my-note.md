## Learn

Like most personal projects, PLFM is partly a learning ground: a way to explore new tools and patterns while building something practical.

## Technologies

- **Flutter**: building the on-device agent and client experience.
- **Go CLI**: experimenting with fast, simple command-line tooling.
- **Networking**: syncing data between a nearby phone, PC, and local services.
- **Hugo**: building nice hugo sites

## Domains

- **Personal finance management**: collecting, organizing, and understanding transaction activity.
- **Personal accounting**: turning raw transaction data into useful records, summaries, and insights.
